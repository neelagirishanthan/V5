Service terms

How we work together

These are the terms in our standard wedding services agreement: what we deliver for each service, and what we ask of you. Your own agreement adds your ceremonies, dates, menus and prices from your quote, and we both sign it before planning begins.

On this page 1 · Food 2 · Decor 3 · Photography 4 · Event item rentals 5 · Garlands 6 · Grandeur 7 · Accommodation On the day General

1Food

We arrange catering for each event to the menus agreed in your agreement. Food is prepared fresh on the day and served by our catering team. Typical menus look like this:

Haldi
  • Veg curry & veg fry
  • Flavoured rice
  • Dal / Rasam / Sambar
  • White rice, curd
  • Papad, fryums
Pelli Koduku & Kooturu
  • Veg curry & veg fry
  • Flavoured rice, roti
  • Dal, Rasam / Sambar
  • White rice, curd
  • Papad, fryums
Pelli (Wedding)
  • Non-veg starters & biryanis
  • Veg starters & biryani
  • Non-veg curry
  • Veg curry, roti / naan
  • Kids' dishes, dessert, ice cream
  1. You confirm the exact dishes at least 14 days before the first event.
  2. You confirm the final guest count for each event at least 7 days before that event. Food is prepared for the confirmed count.
  3. Veg and non-veg dishes are cooked, stored and served separately, with clearly labelled counters.
  4. Changes to the menu or guest count after these dates are made where possible and may change the price. Any change in price is agreed with you in writing first.

2Decor

  1. We introduce you to our decor vendor and share photos of their previous work, so you can choose the look for each event.
  2. Together we finalise the design, colours and items for each event within your agreed decor budget. The final decor plan is shared in writing before work starts.
  3. If your choices would take the decor over budget, we tell you before any extra cost is incurred and revisit the budget with you. Nothing extra is charged without your written approval.
  4. We and the decor vendor set up the decor before each event and take it down afterwards.

3Photography

  1. We introduce you to our vendor photographer. You can review their portfolio and meet them before deciding.
  2. Once you choose the photographer, the coverage (events, hours, photo and video), deliverables and delivery timeline are confirmed in writing as part of your agreement.
  3. We share the event schedule with the photographer and coordinate them on each event day.

4Event item rentals

  1. We provide rented event items such as tables, chairs, linens and other essentials. The list of items for each event is set out in your agreement.
  2. We deliver, set up and collect all rented items.
  3. Damage to rented items beyond normal use, or items lost during the events, is charged at the vendor's cost.

5Garlands

  1. We introduce you to our garland vendor and share samples of their work, so you can choose the flowers and styles.
  2. You confirm the type and number of garlands for each ceremony.
  3. Garlands are made fresh and delivered to the venue on the day of each event.

6Grandeur

  1. We plan the special touches that make your celebrations grand, such as entrances, and share the plan with you in writing.
  2. The plan is finalised within your agreed budget. Anything over budget is discussed and approved by you first.

7Accommodation

  1. We show you rental spaces near your event venues, with the number of guests each can host, so you can choose where your guests stay.
  2. You share the list of staying guests and the room plan at least 14 days before check-in.
  3. Damages: you are responsible for any damage to the rental spaces caused during the stay. Damage costs are charged as billed by the property.
  4. Guests follow each property's house rules, including check-in and check-out times.

On the day of each event

No last-minute hassle. Our team is on site from setup to close, so your family can enjoy the celebrations.

  1. We are on site with our helpers from setup until the end of each event.
  2. We coordinate all vendors (catering, decor, photography, garlands, rentals) and keep each event on schedule.
  3. We take care of all event items: setup, arrangement during the event, and packing up afterwards.
  4. You have one V5 coordinator as your point of contact on each event day.

General

  1. Changes: any change to services, menus or budget is agreed in writing (email or WhatsApp is enough) by both sides.
  2. Vendors: we manage the vendors named in your agreement and are your point of contact for them.
  3. Payment and cancellation: the payment schedule and cancellation terms are set out in your agreement.
