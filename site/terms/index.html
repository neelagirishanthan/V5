Standard Service Terms

These terms explain what V5 Events delivers for each service we provide, what we ask of our clients, and how we agree changes. They apply to every event we plan, from weddings to birthdays and corporate events.

About these terms

  1. In these terms, "V5", "we" and "us" mean V5 Events, and "you" means the client who books our services.
  2. These terms apply to every event we plan for you, including weddings and their ceremonies, birthday parties and corporate events.
  3. Your event agreement sets out the details for your celebration: the events, dates, venues, guest counts, menus, vendors, prices, payment schedule and cancellation terms. If your agreement and these terms differ, your agreement applies.
  4. When you submit our intake form, you confirm that you have read these terms. Both of us sign your event agreement before planning begins.
2

Food and catering

  1. We arrange catering for each event to the menus in your agreement. Food is prepared fresh on the day and served by our catering team. Sample wedding menus are in Schedule A.
  2. You confirm the exact dishes at least 14 days before the first event.
  3. You confirm the final guest count for each event at least 7 days before that event. Food is prepared for the confirmed count.
  4. Vegetarian and non-vegetarian dishes are cooked, stored and served separately, with clearly labelled counters.
  5. Changes to the menu or guest count after these dates are made where possible and may change the price. Any change in price is agreed with you in writing first.
3

Decor

Including mandaps, stages, floral work, lighting, themed setups and kids' play areas.

  1. We introduce you to our decor vendor and share photos of their previous work, so you can choose the look for each event.
  2. Together we finalise the design, colours and items for each event within your agreed decor budget. The final decor plan is shared with you in writing before work starts.
  3. If your choices would take the decor over budget, we tell you before any extra cost is incurred and revisit the budget with you. No extra cost is charged without your written approval.
  4. We and our decor vendor set up the decor before each event and take it down afterwards.
4

Photography

  1. We introduce you to our vendor photographer. You can review their portfolio and meet them before deciding.
  2. Once you choose the photographer, the coverage (events, hours, photo and video), deliverables and delivery timeline are confirmed in writing in your agreement.
  3. We share the event schedule with the photographer and coordinate them on each event day.
5

Event item rentals

  1. We provide rented event items such as tables, chairs, linens and other essentials. The list of items for each event is set out in your agreement.
  2. We deliver, set up and collect all rented items.
  3. Damage to rented items beyond normal use, or items lost during the events, is charged to you at the vendor's cost.
6

Garlands and flowers

  1. We introduce you to our garland vendor and share samples of their work, so you can choose the flowers and styles.
  2. You confirm the type and number of garlands for each ceremony.
  3. Garlands are made fresh and delivered to the venue on the day of each event.
7

Grandeur

  1. We plan the special touches that make your celebration grand, such as entrances, and share the plan with you in writing.
  2. The plan is finalised within your agreed budget. Anything over budget is discussed with you and approved by you first.
8

Guest accommodation

  1. We show you rental spaces near your event venues, with the number of guests each can host, so you can choose where your guests stay.
  2. You share the list of staying guests and the room plan at least 14 days before check-in.
  3. Damages. You are responsible for any damage to the rental spaces caused during the stay. Damage costs are charged to you as billed by the property.
  4. Guests follow each property's house rules, including check-in and check-out times.
9

On the event day

  1. We are on site with our helpers from setup until the end of each event.
  2. We coordinate all vendors, including catering, decor, photography, garlands and rentals, and keep each event on schedule.
  3. We take care of all event items: setup, arrangement during the event, and packing up afterwards.
  4. You have one V5 coordinator as your point of contact on each event day, named in your agreement.
10

Changes and approvals

  1. Any change to services, menus, guest counts or budget is agreed in writing by both of us. A message by email or WhatsApp is enough.
  2. We manage the vendors named in your agreement and are your point of contact for them.
11

Payment and cancellation

  1. Prices, the payment schedule and cancellation terms are set out in your event agreement, following the quote we send you.
