The events

Six ceremonies, one team

Choose the events you're celebrating. We plan each one on its own terms: its guests, decor, menu, music and rituals.

1

Haldi

Turmeric paste for the bride and groom, with marigolds, garlands and the family close by.

2

Pelli Koduku & Kooturu

The ceremonies that make the groom and bride ready for the wedding, with the priest and fresh flowers.

3

Sangeeth

An evening of music and dance: stage, lighting, DJ, bar and live counters.

4

Mehandi & Bangles

Henna for the bride and guests, with the gaajulu ceremony, seating and a backdrop for photos.

5

Pelli & Reception

The muhurtham at the mandapam, then the reception: full catering, decor and photo coverage.

6

Vratham

Satyanarayana Swamy Vratham after the wedding, with the priest, a veg meal and simple floral decor.

What we cover

Everything your wedding needs

We work with trusted vendors for each service. You see their work and choose, and we manage them for you.

Food

Catering for every event with veg and non-veg menus, cooked and served separately, plus live counters.

Decor

Mandapam, stage, floral, lighting and backdrops from our decor vendor, finalised within your budget.

Photography

Photo and video coverage. Meet our photographer, review their portfolio and decide.

Event item rentals

Tables, chairs, linens and event essentials, delivered, set up and collected.

Garlands

Fresh flower garlands for each ceremony from our garland vendor, made on the day.

Grandeur

Grand entrances and special touches that make the big moments memorable.

Accommodation

Rental stays near your venues, matched to how many guests each place can host.

On the day

Our team and helpers are on site from setup to close. No last-minute hassle.
